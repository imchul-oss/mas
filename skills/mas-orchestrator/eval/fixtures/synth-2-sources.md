# Three sources on one number: 2025 global installed grid-battery storage

## Source A - national energy agency statistical release (2026-03)
Installed grid-scale battery storage reached **186 GW** worldwide at end-2025.
Method: sum of member-state regulatory filings, commissioned capacity only.
Excludes behind-the-meter residential systems. Revision policy: figures restated
annually as late filings arrive; the 2024 figure was revised upward 4% after
first publication.

## Source B - industry association annual outlook (2026-02)
Global battery storage stood at **242 GW** at end-2025.
Method: member survey plus modelled estimates for non-responding markets.
Includes behind-the-meter residential and commercial systems. The association's
members are storage manufacturers and developers.

## Source C - consultancy market note (2026-05)
Total energy storage capacity: **511 GW** at end-2025, up 31% year on year.
Method: proprietary database. A footnote states the total covers "all energy
storage technologies", and a chart on the following page shows pumped hydro as
roughly 60% of the total.
