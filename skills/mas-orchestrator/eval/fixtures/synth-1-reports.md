# Five analyst reports on the same question: 2026 H2 outlook for enterprise AI spend

## Report 1 - large investment bank, equity research (2026-06)
Enterprise AI budgets grow 34% in H2. Basis: a 400-CIO survey run in April.
Notes that 61% of respondents describe current spend as "pilot or proof of
concept". Bank has advisory relationships with three of the named vendors.

## Report 2 - boutique research firm (2026-07)
Growth decelerates to 12%. Basis: observed cloud commit drawdowns across 90
enterprise contracts. Argues survey-based figures overstate intent because CIOs
report planned rather than executed budget.

## Report 3 - vendor-sponsored study (2026-05)
Growth of 51%, and 78% of enterprises report positive ROI. The sponsoring vendor
sells the category being measured. Sample of 200, recruited from the sponsor's
customer base.

## Report 4 - academic working paper, not peer reviewed (2026-06)
Finds no statistically significant relationship between AI spend and productivity
at the firm level over 2024-2025, n=1,200 firms, but notes the measurement window
is likely too short for capital-deepening effects to appear.

## Report 5 - trade press aggregation (2026-07)
Reports "consensus of roughly 30% growth" by averaging four published forecasts,
three of which are Reports 1, 2 and 3 above.
